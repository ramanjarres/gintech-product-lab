# Problem Statement

## Initial Problem

Many small and medium businesses collect information from different sources such as customer feedback, sales records, operational reports, WhatsApp conversations, surveys, support interactions, and manual spreadsheets.

However, this information is often fragmented, difficult to analyze, and not directly connected to decision-making.

As a result, business owners and teams may struggle to understand:

- What problems are affecting customers
- Which issues happen most often
- Which problems have the highest business impact
- Where process inefficiencies are happening
- What should be prioritized first
- Whether improvements are actually working

## Why This Matters

When businesses cannot clearly connect data with decisions, they may waste time and resources solving low-impact problems while ignoring issues that affect revenue, customer experience, or operational efficiency.

This creates opportunities for:

- Poor customer experience
- Operational waste
- Repeated errors
- Slow decision-making
- Missed revenue opportunities
- Weak prioritization
- Lack of visibility into business performance

## Initial Hypothesis

A software solution that combines structured data collection, customer feedback analysis, operational metrics, prioritization methods, and AI/ML-supported insights could help businesses make better decisions and focus on the problems that matter most.

## Potential Users

- Small and medium business owners
- Operations managers
- Customer experience teams
- Product teams
- Sales and commercial teams
- Process improvement teams
- Data analysts
- Consultants working with business improvement

## Initial Product Opportunity

Build a platform or tool that helps businesses collect, organize, analyze, and prioritize customer and operational problems using product thinking, data analysis, Lean Six Sigma, and machine learning experimentation.
