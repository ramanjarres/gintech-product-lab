# Gintech Product Lab — Research Synthesis 01–08

## Purpose

This document synthesizes the findings from the first eight case studies
analyzed by Gintech Product Lab.

The objective is not to select a final product or market.

The objective is to identify:

- Repeated business problems
- Common data patterns
- Decision points
- Technical patterns
- Regional evidence
- Product opportunities
- Remaining assumptions
- Research gaps

The findings in this document represent research hypotheses.

They should not be interpreted as proof of product-market fit.

---

# 2. The Strongest Pattern Across All Cases

The most important pattern identified across the eight cases is:

Existing Business Data
        ↓
Understand / Detect / Predict
        ↓
Business Context
        ↓
Decision
        ↓
Operational Action
        ↓
Business Outcome

The industries are different.

The datasets are different.

The algorithms are different.

But the fundamental business pattern remains similar.

The value does not come primarily from the algorithm.

The value comes from improving a business decision.

Examples:

Airbnb:
Customer messages

Ricoh:
Incident records and descriptions

Nestlé:
Historical demand and sales information

SENER:
Machine telemetry

AI4Time:
SAP PM maintenance records

Megatiendas:
Historical sales and promotional information

Vanti:
Customer PQRS

Héctor Ocaña:
Historical demand and logistics information

This suggests an important hypothesis:

Organizations may not always need more data.

They may need better ways to use the data they already generate.

# 6. Prediction Is Not the Final Product

Several cases demonstrate an important distinction.

A prediction may say:

Expected demand = 420 units.

But the business decision may be:

Purchase 150 additional units.

A model may say:

Negative sentiment.

But the business decision may be:

Investigate a recurring billing problem affecting a customer segment.

A model may say:

High probability of equipment failure.

But the business decision may be:

Inspect Asset 17 before the next production cycle.

Therefore:

Prediction
≠
Decision

A potential Gintech opportunity exists between these two layers.

Prediction
        ↓
Business Context
        ↓
Impact
        ↓
Recommendation
        ↓
Decision

---

# 7. The Dashboard Is Not the Product

Multiple cases include dashboards or interfaces.

However, the value originates from the analytical system behind the
interface.

For example:

AI4Time:

SAP PM
→ ETL
→ Reliability Analysis
→ Machine Learning
→ Prediction
→ Power BI

The Power BI dashboard is the interface.

The intelligence exists in the analytical pipeline.

Therefore:

Gintech should not position itself primarily as a dashboard company.

Dashboards may become one delivery mechanism for Gintech intelligence.

---

# 8. AI Is Not the Value Proposition

None of the strongest business problems begin with:

"We need artificial intelligence."

They begin with questions such as:

Why are customers complaining?

How much inventory will we need?

Which machine requires maintenance?

Should we dispatch a technician?

How should vehicles be allocated?

Therefore, Gintech should begin with:

Business Problem
        ↓
Decision
        ↓
Required Data
        ↓
Analytical Method

rather than:

AI Model
        ↓
Search for a Problem

A weak product would provide:

Frequency increase: +38%
Severity: High
Trend: Increasing

Investigate billing workflow.

The potential differentiation is therefore not:

"Analyze customer feedback."

"Transform customer feedback into prioritized business problems."

## Customer Intelligence Advantages

Potential advantages include:

- Data is commonly available
- Relatively low infrastructure requirements
- Fast MVP development
- Easy integration with text-based sources
- Applicable across multiple industries

---

## Customer Intelligence Risks

Potential risks include:

- Sentiment analysis is increasingly commoditized
- Generic LLM solutions can perform basic text analysis
- Competitive differentiation may be difficult
- Business value must extend beyond classification

Therefore, Gintech would need to connect customer signals to:

Problem
→ Impact
→ Priority
→ Action

engineering.

Data Quality
        ↓

        ↓

        ↓

There is a risk of making Gintech too broad.

A platform capable of solving:

Customer Intelligence
+
Demand Intelligence

# 24. Evidence Hierarchy Going Forward

Future Gintech research should distinguish between different levels of
evidence.

### Level 1 — Assumption

"We believe companies have this problem."

Weak evidence.

### Level 2 — Secondary Evidence

Research papers, industry reports and case studies show that the problem
exists.

This is primarily where the current research sits.

### Level 3 — Customer Evidence

Multiple potential customers confirm the same problem and explain how they
currently solve it.

Stronger evidence.

### Level 4 — Data Evidence

A potential customer provides real or anonymized data and Gintech can
demonstrate the problem quantitatively.

Very strong evidence.

### Level 5 — Behavioral Evidence

A company agrees to participate in a pilot or invest employee time and data.

Stronger evidence.

### Level 6 — Commercial Evidence

A company pays for the solution.

Strongest evidence of market demand.

# 28. Current Working Thesis

The strongest working thesis emerging from the research is:

> Businesses already generate valuable data through their normal operations,
> but often lack the analytical layer required to transform that information
> into timely decisions.

Gintech could potentially operate in that layer.

Conceptually:

BUSINESS SYSTEMS
        ↓
       DATA
        ↓
     GINTECH
        ↓
   INTELLIGENCE
        ↓
 RECOMMENDATION
        ↓
 BUSINESS DECISION
        ↓
     OUTCOME

This thesis still requires market validation.

# 29. Current Product Principle

Do not begin by asking:

"What AI can we build?"

Begin by asking:

"What recurring business decision is currently expensive, slow, uncertain or
poorly informed?"

Then determine:

What data exists?
        ↓
What intelligence is missing?
        ↓
What decision could improve?
        ↓
Can Gintech demonstrate measurable value?
