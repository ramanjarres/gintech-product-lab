# CCB APM Opportunity Analysis — 2026

## Opportunity
Cámara de Comercio de Barranquilla — SaaS de Observabilidad y Monitoreo de Rendimiento de Aplicaciones (APM)

## Status
**Decision: NO-GO as prime contractor**  
**Strategic value: HIGH**

## Why this opportunity matters
This opportunity is highly aligned with Gintech's long-term direction in observability, digital experience, analytics, automation, AI-assisted operations, dashboards and business metrics. However, Gintech currently does not meet the formal eligibility requirements.

## Scope
The CCB is seeking a SaaS platform and implementation partner for application performance monitoring, infrastructure monitoring, logs, metrics and traces, OpenTelemetry, RUM, synthetic monitoring, anomaly detection, AI-assisted root cause analysis, automation workflows, SIEM/ITSM integrations, business and technical dashboards, SLA/SLO monitoring, 24x7x365 support, transition/migration and knowledge transfer.

## Base environment
- Oracle SOA Suite 12c
- Oracle Database 12c
- IBM Power9 / AIX
- Laserfiche
- Microsoft SQL Server
- AWS App Runner
- MariaDB
- AWS

## Mandatory requirements
### Vendor / platform
- Provider must be authorized to commercialize, implement or support the proposed solution.
- If operating as a partner or reseller, a current manufacturer certification is required.
- At least one technical resource must hold an official certification for the proposed platform.

### Support
- 24x7x365 critical support
- Severity 1 response: <=15 min
- Manufacturer escalation: <=30 min
- On-site specialist during the critical renewal period in Barranquilla

### SLA
- SaaS availability >=99.9%
- Initial implementation <=30 calendar days

## Experience barrier
The provider must demonstrate:
- Minimum 3 years of relevant experience
- 3 completed contracts in the last 3 years
- Each contract must have a value equal to or greater than the submitted offer
- Formal client certifications are required

Consortiums, temporary unions and joint ventures are not accepted.

## Evaluation model
| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Economic offer | 45% |
| Technical compliance | 30% |
| Experience | 20% |
| Local provider | 5% |

Local provider means principal domicile in Atlántico.

## Commercial estimate
### Estimated 12-month contract
**COP $200M – $300M**  
Reference scenario: **~COP $239M + VAT**

### Estimated 24-month contract
**COP $350M – $500M**  
Reference scenario: **~COP $419M + VAT**

These estimates are directional and depend heavily on telemetry volume, chosen APM platform, licensing model, data retention, implementation effort, support structure and migration complexity.

## Decision
### Gintech today
**NO-GO**

Main blockers:
- no 3-year comparable track record
- no 3 comparable contracts
- no manufacturer authorization
- no certified platform resource
- no 24x7 support operation
- consortium / JV not allowed

## Strategic implications
This opportunity shows what institutional buyers expect from enterprise technology providers. Gintech should build toward this capability through manufacturer partnerships, technical certifications, certified client projects, formal support processes, security and confidentiality controls, vendor-readiness documentation, financial history and insurability.

## Long-term target
### 0–12 months
- Build platform partnerships
- Obtain technical certifications
- Execute smaller observability / analytics projects

### 12–24 months
- Accumulate certified customer references
- Develop support and implementation capability

### 24–36 months
- Become eligible for opportunities of this size

## Key lesson
Every early Gintech project should be designed not only to generate revenue, but also to create verifiable experience, client certifications, repeatable delivery capability and institutional credibility.
