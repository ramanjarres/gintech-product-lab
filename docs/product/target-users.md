# Target Users

## Purpose

This document defines the initial target users for Gintech Product Lab. The goal is to understand who could benefit from a software, data science, and AI/ML-supported solution that helps businesses identify problems, analyze information, prioritize opportunities, and improve decision-making.

## Primary User Groups

### 1. Small and Medium Business Owners

Business owners who manage daily operations, customer service, sales, and process decisions but do not always have structured tools to analyze customer feedback, operational data, and business performance.

#### Main Problems

- Information is spread across WhatsApp, spreadsheets, sales records, customer conversations, and manual reports.
- Decisions are often based on intuition instead of structured data.
- It is difficult to know which problems should be solved first.
- Customer complaints or operational issues may repeat without being clearly measured.
- There is limited visibility into customer experience and process performance.

#### Needs

- Simple dashboards and summaries.
- Clear identification of recurring problems.
- Prioritization of improvement opportunities.
- Better understanding of customer friction.
- Practical recommendations based on business impact.

---

### 2. Operations Managers

People responsible for ensuring that business processes work efficiently and that customer-impacting problems are identified and resolved.

#### Main Problems

- Operational issues are not always documented consistently.
- Teams may work reactively instead of proactively.
- There is limited visibility into process bottlenecks.
- It is difficult to connect operational failures with customer or revenue impact.

#### Needs

- Process visibility.
- Issue tracking.
- Root-cause analysis support.
- Operational metrics.
- Prioritization based on impact and urgency.
- Follow-up of improvement actions.

---

### 3. Customer Experience Teams

Teams responsible for understanding customer pain points, analyzing feedback, and improving customer journeys.

#### Main Problems

- Customer feedback may be unstructured.
- Complaints are collected but not always categorized.
- Patterns across journeys are difficult to identify manually.
- Customer sentiment may not be connected to operational or business data.

#### Needs

- Feedback classification.
- Journey-based analysis.
- Voice of Customer insights.
- Sentiment and recurring topic detection.
- Prioritization of customer pain points.

---

### 4. Product Teams

Product Owners, Product Managers, and product teams that need to understand customer problems, prioritize backlog items, and validate whether product changes are solving real needs.

#### Main Problems

- Product decisions may be disconnected from customer feedback or operational evidence.
- Backlogs may contain many items without clear impact scoring.
- Teams may struggle to prioritize between customer issues, business needs, and technical constraints.
- It can be difficult to validate if a released improvement actually solved the problem.

#### Needs

- Problem discovery support.
- Customer and business impact analysis.
- Prioritization framework.
- User stories and acceptance criteria based on real problems.
- Post-implementation validation.

---

### 5. Data Analysts and Business Analysts

Analysts who collect, clean, analyze, and communicate data to support business decisions.

#### Main Problems

- Data is fragmented across multiple sources.
- Manual reporting consumes time.
- Business stakeholders may need insights but not raw data.
- Analysis may not always lead to clear action.

#### Needs

- Structured data sources.
- Exploratory data analysis.
- Business intelligence dashboards.
- Clear problem categorization.
- Insight-to-action documentation.

---

### 6. Consultants and Process Improvement Teams

Consultants or internal improvement teams working with business diagnosis, operational efficiency, customer experience, or Lean Six Sigma initiatives.

#### Main Problems

- Diagnosing business problems requires collecting information from many sources.
- Improvement opportunities need to be prioritized.
- Clients may not have mature data systems.
- It can be difficult to measure before-and-after impact.

#### Needs

- Business problem mapping.
- DMAIC support.
- Process performance indicators.
- Root-cause analysis documentation.
- Improvement tracking.

## Initial Target Segment

The initial target segment for this project will be:

**Small and medium businesses with customer-facing operations that collect feedback, sales, or operational information but lack a structured way to convert that information into decisions and improvement priorities.**

Examples may include:

- Restaurants
- Retail businesses
- Service-based businesses
- Local logistics or delivery operations
- Customer support operations
- Small e-commerce businesses

## Early Adopter Profile

An early adopter could be a business owner or operations leader who:

- Already receives customer complaints or feedback.
- Uses WhatsApp, spreadsheets, forms, or manual reports.
- Wants to improve customer experience.
- Does not have a mature business intelligence system.
- Needs help prioritizing problems.
- Is open to using software and data to improve decisions.

## Key Assumption

The project assumes that many businesses do not need a complex enterprise platform at first. They need a practical tool that helps them organize information, identify patterns, prioritize problems, and make better decisions.

This assumption must be validated through research, interviews, surveys, and data analysis.
